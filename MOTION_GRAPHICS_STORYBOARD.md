## 🎨 Motion Design Production Settings (For After Effects / Premiere / Blender)

If you or a motion designer are working in Adobe After Effects or Premiere Pro:

* **Composition Settings:** 1920 × 1080 (or 3840 × 2160), 60.00 fps, Duration 01:12:00.
* **Color Space:** Rec.709 16-bit / 32-bit float.
* **Keyframe Easing:** 
  * Text reveals: `Cubic-Bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (snappy ease-out).
  * 3D card flap drop: `Cubic-Bezier(0.215, 0.61, 0.355, 1)`.
* **Motion Blur:** Shutter Angle: 180°, Shutter Phase: -90°.
* **Music Recommendation:** Search "Minimalist Tech Product Launch" or "Modern Ambient Future Bass" at 124 BPM.
